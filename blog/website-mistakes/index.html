5 Website Mistakes That Are Quietly Costing You Leads

Web Design Jul 28, 2026 · 5 min read

You can run great ads and rank well on Google — but if your website loses visitors before they take action, all that traffic goes to waste. Here are the five most common issues we find when auditing client sites.

1. Slow load times

Every extra second a page takes to load pushes more visitors away, especially on mobile. Large uncompressed images and heavy scripts are usually the biggest culprits.

2. No clear call-to-action

If a visitor lands on your homepage and can't immediately tell what you want them to do next — call, book, buy — they'll leave without doing anything at all.

3. Contact forms that don't work reliably

Broken or overly long forms quietly kill more leads than businesses realize. Keep required fields minimal and always test the actual submission, not just the design.

4. Poor mobile experience

Most traffic today is mobile. Tiny buttons, unreadable text, or layouts that break on smaller screens are enough to make visitors give up before converting.

5. No trust signals

Missing reviews, testimonials, or clear contact information makes visitors hesitant to commit. A few visible trust signals near your call-to-action can meaningfully lift conversions.

  • Run your site through a free page speed test
  • Submit your own contact form to confirm it works end-to-end
  • Check your homepage on an actual phone, not just a resized browser

The bottom line

Fixing these issues usually costs far less than the extra ad spend it takes to make up for the leads they're silently losing. A quick audit is often the highest-ROI thing you can do this month.
